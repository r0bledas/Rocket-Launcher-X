Privacy Policy

Rocket Launcher / Rocket Launcher X · Last updated: 17 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile application Rocket Launcher — also published under the name Rocket Launcher X (the same application, “the App”, “we”, “us”) — collects, uses, and protects information. Where required by law, we ask for your consent separately (for advertising, tracking, and optionally for analytics); merely using the App is not treated as consent to those optional activities.

1. Summary

  • We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, precise location, or the contents of your device.
  • Usage analytics is off by default and opt-in. When enabled, it sends the app name and full URL scheme you launch, the widget/slot, and the local hour of day under a pseudonymous install ID.
  • The App shows ads through Google AdMob. With your permission ads may be personalized; otherwise they are non-personalized.
  • You can withdraw consent and reset/delete data from Settings → Privacy.

3. Information We Collect

This section is the authoritative inventory and is updated whenever the App’s privacy, advertising, or monetization behavior changes.

3.1 Information you configure (kept on your device)

  • App names, app icons, URL schemes you add to widgets, and appearance settings (colors, fonts, alignment).
  • Stored locally and in the App’s shared container so widgets can read it. It is not transmitted to us. However, the app name you enter — whether chosen from the catalog or typed as a custom title — may be sent to Apple’s iTunes Search API to fetch its icon, unless you disable “Fetch app icons from Apple” in Settings.
  • An optional launch background image you choose is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to us.

3.2 Usage analytics (pseudonymous, opt-in)

  • Off by default. Nothing is sent until you turn on “Share anonymous usage data”. Our legal basis is your consent.
  • When enabled: an app-launch event with the app name and full URL scheme (including custom schemes you type), the widget number and slot, the local hour of day, the launch source (widget or in-app), and basic device context (device model, OS version, app version, region).
  • A random install identifier (a persistent identifier) stored in the Keychain on this device only. It is not your identity and is not restored from backups.
  • Reset it any time with “Reset analytics ID” in Settings. To request access to or deletion of your analytics data, copy the ID shown in Settings and email it to raudel@raudel.lat.
  • Sensitive data: because app names and URL schemes can reveal which apps you use, this information is sent only when you explicitly opt in. You can turn it off at any time.
  • Our self-hosted analytics server necessarily sees the IP address of the request; handling: [[IP_HANDLING]].
  • Current build: analytics is not active in the shipped version (the analytics endpoint is not configured), so no analytics data leaves your device today.

3.3 Advertising

  • The App uses Google AdMob for banner ads and optional rewarded ads (for example, to unlock a widget slot for a limited time).
  • Where permitted — in the EEA/UK via Google’s consent form, and on iOS only if you allow it through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt — ads may be personalized. Google may then collect your advertising identifier (IDFA), advertising data, and related device information.
  • If you decline tracking or do not consent, ads are non-personalized. See policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3.4 Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We receive only whether an entitlement is active; we do not receive your payment details.

3.5 What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise geolocation, health data, messages, or any personal content.

4. How We Use Information

  • To understand aggregate usage and improve features and reliability (analytics, with consent).
  • To display ads (personalized only with your consent/permission).
  • To verify entitlements for purchased or rewarded features.
