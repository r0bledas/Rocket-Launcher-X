Privacy Policy

Rocket Launcher · Last updated: 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Rocket Launcher app (“the App”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, and protects information. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Summary

  • We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, precise location, or the contents of your device.
  • We collect anonymous, pseudonymous usage statistics (e.g., which app slots are launched and when) to improve the App.
  • The App shows ads through Google AdMob. With your permission, ads may be personalized; otherwise they are non-personalized.
  • You can turn off usage sharing at any time in the App: Settings → Privacy → Share anonymous usage data.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Usage Analytics (anonymous / pseudonymous)

  • App-launch events: which catalogued app was opened, the launch source (widget or in-app), and a coarse timestamp.
  • A random install identifier stored locally in the Keychain (not your identity, resettable by reinstalling).
  • Basic device context: device model, operating-system version, app version, and region.
  • We do not log raw custom URL schemes you enter (they are mapped to a catalogued app or a generic “custom” bucket).

2.2 Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob. Where permitted (for example in the EEA/UK, via the consent form) and, on iOS, only if you allow tracking through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt, ads may be personalized and Google may use your advertising identifier (IDFA) and related advertising data. If you decline, ads are served on a non-personalized basis. See Google’s policy: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

2.3 Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

3. How We Use Information

  • To understand aggregate usage and improve features and reliability.
  • To display ads (personalized only with your consent/permission).
  • To verify entitlements for purchased features.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share limited data with service providers strictly to operate the App: Google (AdMob) for advertising, and our self-hosted analytics server for anonymous usage statistics. Data may be processed in countries other than yours.

5. Legal Bases (GDPR) and Your Rights

Where GDPR applies, we process analytics on the basis of legitimate interest and advertising on the basis of consent. You may access, correct, delete, or object to processing, and withdraw consent at any time via the in-app consent form or by contacting us. California residents may opt out of the “sale”/“sharing” of personal information, which we do not perform beyond advertising as described.

6. Data Retention

Anonymous analytics are retained in aggregate for a limited period. The local install identifier is stored until you reinstall or reset the App.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by the “Last updated” date above.
