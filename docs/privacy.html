Privacy policy

Rocket Launcher / Rocket Launcher X · Last updated: 17 October 2026

This privacy policy explains how the mobile application Rocket Launcher, also published under the name Rocket Launcher X (together, the app), collects, uses and protects information. Where required by law, we request your consent separately for advertising, tracking and analytics. Mere use of the app does not constitute consent to those optional activities.

1. Summary

  • We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photographs, precise location or the contents of your device.
  • Usage analytics is enabled by default and may be turned off at any time. It records the name and full URL scheme of the app you launch, the widget and slot, and the local hour of day, under a pseudonymous installation identifier.
  • The app displays advertising through Google AdMob. Advertising may be personalised with your permission and is otherwise non-personalised.
  • You may withdraw consent and reset or delete data from Settings → Privacy.

3. Information we collect

This section constitutes the authoritative inventory of the information we process and is updated whenever the privacy, advertising or monetisation behaviour of the app changes.

3.1 Information you configure (kept on your device)

  • App names, app icons, URL schemes you add to widgets, and appearance settings (colours, fonts and alignment).
  • Such information is stored locally and in the shared container of the app so that widgets may read it. It is not transmitted to us. However, the app name you enter, whether selected from the catalogue or typed as a custom title, may be sent to the Apple iTunes Search API to retrieve its icon, unless you disable “Fetch app icons from Apple” in Settings.
  • An optional launch background image that you choose is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to us.

3.2 Usage analytics (pseudonymous, enabled by default)

  • Analytics is enabled by default. You may turn it off at any time by disabling “Share anonymous usage data” in Settings → Privacy. Where local law requires consent for analytics, disabling analytics withdraws that consent.
  • While analytics is enabled, an app-launch event records the app name and full URL scheme (including custom schemes you type), the widget number and slot, the local hour of day, the launch source (widget or in-app), and basic device context (device model, operating-system version, app version and region).
  • A random installation identifier (a persistent identifier) is stored in the Keychain on this device only. It does not identify you and is not restored from backups.
  • You may reset the installation identifier at any time by selecting “Reset analytics ID” in Settings. To request access to or deletion of your analytics data, copy the identifier displayed in Settings and send it to raudel@raudel.lat.
  • Because app names and URL schemes may reveal which apps you use, such information is processed only for the purposes described in this policy. Analytics is enabled by default and may be turned off at any time.
  • Our self-hosted analytics server necessarily receives the IP address of the request. Handling: [[IP_HANDLING]].
  • In the current build, analytics is not active because the analytics endpoint is not configured; consequently, no analytics data leaves your device at this time.

3.3 Advertising

  • The app uses Google AdMob for banner advertising and optional rewarded advertising, for example to unlock a widget slot for a limited period.
  • Where permitted, including in the EEA and the United Kingdom through Google’s consent form and, on iOS, only where you permit it through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt, advertising may be personalised. Google may then collect your advertising identifier (IDFA), advertising data and related device information.
  • If you decline tracking or do not consent, advertising is non-personalised. See policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3.4 Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We receive only whether an entitlement is active; we do not receive your payment details.

3.5 Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, telephone number, contacts, photographs, precise geolocation, health data, messages or any personal content.

4. How we use information

  • To understand aggregate usage and to improve features and reliability (analytics, enabled by default and subject to opt-out).
  • To display advertising, which is personalised only with your consent or permission.
  • To verify entitlements for purchased or rewarded features.

11. Apple Watch app

The optional Apple Watch companion stores your recent search terms locally on the watch (up to five) and opens web searches in the watch browser, sending your search text to Google when you search. You may clear the history from the Watch app. The watch app does not collect analytics.
