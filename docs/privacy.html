Privacy Policy

Rocket Launcher / Rocket Launcher X · Last updated: 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile application Rocket Launcher — also published under the name Rocket Launcher X (the same application, referred to together as “the App”, “we”, “us”) — collects, uses, and protects information. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Summary

  • We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, precise location, or the contents of your device.
  • We collect anonymous, pseudonymous usage statistics (e.g., which app slots are launched and when) to improve the App.
  • The App shows ads through Google AdMob. With your permission, ads may be personalized; otherwise they are non-personalized.
  • You can turn off usage sharing at any time in the App: Settings → Privacy → Share anonymous usage data.

2. Information We Collect

This section is the authoritative list of what the App collects. It is updated whenever the App’s privacy, advertising, or monetization behavior changes.

2.1 Information you configure (kept on your device)

  • The app names, app icons, and URL schemes you add to your widgets, and your appearance settings (colors, fonts, alignment).
  • This information is stored locally on your device and in the App’s shared container so widgets can read it. It is not transmitted to us.

2.2 Usage analytics (anonymous / pseudonymous)

  • App-launch events: which catalogued app was opened, the launch source (widget or in-app), and a coarse timestamp.
  • A random install identifier stored locally in the Keychain (not your identity; resettable by reinstalling the App).
  • Basic device context: device model, operating-system version, app version, and region.
  • We do not log the raw custom URL schemes you enter — they are mapped to a catalogued app name/category or a generic “custom” bucket.
  • You can disable this at any time in Settings → Privacy. When disabled, no analytics events are sent.

2.3 Advertising

  • The App uses Google AdMob to display banner ads and optional rewarded ads (for example, to unlock an extra widget slot for a limited time).
  • Where permitted — for example in the EEA/UK via Google’s consent form, and on iOS only if you allow it through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt — ads may be personalized. In that case Google may collect your advertising identifier (IDFA), advertising data (such as the ads you were shown), and related device information.
  • If you decline tracking or do not consent, ads are served on a non-personalized basis.
  • See Google’s policy: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

2.4 Purchases

In-app purchases (including the lifetime bundle and individual features) are processed by Apple. We receive only whether an entitlement is active; we do not receive your payment details.

2.5 What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise geolocation, health data, the content of your messages, or any other personal content.

3. How We Use Information

  • To understand aggregate usage and improve features and reliability.
  • To display ads (personalized only with your consent/permission).
  • To verify entitlements for purchased or rewarded features.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share limited data with service providers strictly to operate the App: Google (AdMob) for advertising, and our self-hosted analytics server for anonymous usage statistics. Data may be processed in countries other than yours.

5. Legal Bases (GDPR) and Your Rights

Where GDPR applies, we process analytics on the basis of legitimate interest and advertising on the basis of consent. You may access, correct, delete, or object to processing, and withdraw consent at any time via the in-app consent form or by contacting us. California residents may opt out of the “sale”/“sharing” of personal information, which we do not perform beyond advertising as described.

6. Data Retention

Anonymous analytics are retained in aggregate for a limited period. The local install identifier is stored until you reinstall or reset the App.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by the “Last updated” date above.
